## Short Description

A tense top-down sci-fi survival shooter where every gunshot can attract something worse.

## Long Description

Fight through eight hostile research facilities in a single-player campaign shaped by scarce supplies, dangerous noise, failing power, and distinct alien hunters. Restore facility systems, choose when to fight, and preserve your progress through resilient campaign saves and checkpoints.

## Verified Feature Copy

- Eight-mission single-player campaign
- Keyboard/mouse, controller, and landscape touch controls
- Five tactical weapons and seven enemy archetypes
- Sound-reactive enemies, dynamic power and lighting, hazards, and access systems
- Explorer, Standard, and Survivor difficulty modes
- Persistent local campaign saves with checkpoint recovery
- Adjustable UI scale, screen shake, flash intensity, aim assist, vibration, and audio
