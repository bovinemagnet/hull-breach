# ADR-0006: Prefer CC0 Prototype Assets

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Prototype art must be replaceable and legally safe without delaying gameplay experiments.

## Decision

Prefer CC0 assets for prototypes. Verify and record every third-party asset before use.

## Consequences

Licensing risk and replacement cost remain low. Asset selection is narrower, and all external content requires provenance in `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md` and, where practical, a local `SOURCE.md`.
