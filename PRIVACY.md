# Privacy

Hull Breach does not require an account, connect to an online service, display advertising, collect analytics or telemetry, or use a backend. The game does not intentionally collect personal information, including from children.

Campaign saves, settings, and diagnostic logs remain on the player's device. The game requests no contacts, location, microphone, photo-library, advertising-ID, or general storage permission. Platform stores may process purchases or account data under their own policies; Hull Breach does not receive that data through the game.

Players may voluntarily submit a bug report at <https://github.com/bovinemagnet/hull-breach/issues>. Reports may include version, platform, mission, checkpoint, and player-provided logs or screenshots. Contributors must not request passwords, signing material, or unrelated personal information.

This policy reflects version 1.0.0-rc.1 as reviewed on 2026-08-25. It must be updated before adding any network, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, account, or store SDK behavior.
