# Privacy

Hull Breach does not require an account, connect to an online service, display advertising, or collect analytics. Campaign saves, settings, and diagnostic logs remain on the player's device unless the player chooses to share them with a tester or maintainer.

Beta bug reports may include the game version, platform, mission, checkpoint, and player-provided logs or screenshots. Contributors must not request passwords, signing material, or unrelated personal information. This statement must be reviewed before any future network, telemetry, crash-reporting, or store SDK is introduced.
